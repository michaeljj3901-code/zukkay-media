# Script drafts + critic/judge pass (reels-af pattern, run by hand, no LLM API calls)
Structure: Hook (first 1-3 s, on-screen hook box) -> Mechanism (2-4 word-burst beats) -> Payoff (one line that echoes a hook keyword so the loop reads back into the opening).
Criteria are reels-af judge priorities: hook strength (<1 s thumb stop), specificity, loop-back, trope avoidance, flow, stake, plus Zukkay retention rules (price + "link in bio" from frame 1, product in frame 1). Each is scored 1-10, and the composite is an overall call, not an average.

| A | Park your controller here. | Off the couch. / Onto the skull. / Printed to order. / Set it down. | PARKED. | 9 | 7 | 9 | 9 | 8 | 7 | **8.5 WINNER** |
| B | Park your controller here. | Red skull. / Melting drips. / Controller on top. | Park it here. | 9 | 8 | 7 | 8 | 7 | 5 | 7.5 |
| C | Where does your controller live? | Couch cushions. / The floor. / Lost again. | Park it here. | 6 | 5 | 8 | 6 | 8 | 7 | 6.5 |
Why A wins: it keeps the proven hook, and its mechanism tells a before/after story (couch, then skull) instead of listing features. The payoff "PARKED." echoes "Park" for the loop. B repeats what's already on screen, and C's question hook is a weaker thumb stop and drops the proven words.
