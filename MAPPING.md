## How these were made

- Every product pixel comes from a real photo of the real print: Michael's originals from ZUKKAY HQ/02 CONTENT LIBRARY (1512x2016 iPhone photos, a 4K video frame), the 12 MP string-art photos on the box, and the live site images.
- Products were cut out locally (rembg BiRefNet, plus color keying so frame windows and lanyard slots stay see-through), and small or soft sources were upscaled locally with Real-ESRGAN x4 (CPU, free).
- Lifestyle backgrounds are AI images from Higgsfield (gpt_image_2_6) generated EMPTY, with no product in them; the real cutout was composited in afterward. So AI never redrew any product.
- AI-generated model: only the nurses-station background (lifestyle1 on all 7 nurse listings) contains a person, an out-of-focus nurse walking in the corridor. Mark those "AI-generated model".
- No text, prices or CTAs are burned into any image. No badge reel appears in any nurse image.
